How Cutoff Times Work

The cutoff time determines when overtime begins. Overtime is calculated for any exit time after the cutoff.

Examples:

  • Cutoff: 17:30, Exit: 19:00 → Overtime: 1.5 hours
  • Cutoff: 17:30, Exit: 16:00 → Overtime: 0 hours
  • Cutoff: 17:30, Exit: 02:00 (next day) → Overnight shift handled correctly
Backup Roles

Backup roles (marked with checkbox) get special overtime calculation on weekends and holidays. All time from call-in (start time) to exit counts as overtime.

Example:

  • Backup role on Saturday, Start: 09:00, Exit: 17:00 → Overtime: 8.0 hours (full shift)
