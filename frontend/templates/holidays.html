How Holidays Affect Overtime

For roles marked as "Backup" roles, residents working on holidays get overtime calculated differently:

  • On weekdays: Normal cutoff-based overtime calculation
  • On weekends or holidays: All time from call-in (start time) to exit counts as overtime
